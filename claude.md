# Project development guide.

## Purpose

This is the personal website of Gareth MacLeod. He is a startup founder and software
engineer based in Waterloo, Ontario, Canada. He would like to increase his public
presence to increase his career opportunities and general level of public influence.

The main feature of this website will be his writing, which is a mixture of tech- and
startup-focused entries, alongside some more political or philosophical musings.

## Stylistic intent

The style of this website is confident understatement. The inspiration is the faculty
pages of some CS professors, who will display some of the most insightful writing in the
medium of unstyled html that could have been written in 1995. A great example of this is
The Bitter Lesson by Richard Sutton http://www.incompleteideas.net/IncIdeas/BitterLesson.html.

We should let the content, and the author's own achievements, speak for themselves. We
have nothing to prove. However, the author is not above a tasteful flourish here and there,
for the dual purposes of self-satisfaction (the author does appreciate beauty and design)
and to hint to the user that this minimalism is a choice.

## Tooling

Since this site is maintained by only a single individual, please keep the architecture
and tooling simple. For the forseeable future this will be a static site, hosted on
github pages or some other simple store.

Use nextjs with typescript. Use tailwind for styling. Deviate only where necessary to
achieve our stylistic intent.
